Introduces fundamental machine learning techniques and their practical applications in finance, exploring how data-driven algorithms enhance decision-making in quantitative trading, credit risk assessment, and fraud detection.

* Schedule: 2025-10-15 to 2026-01-07, every Wednesday, 14:20-17:50
* Location: Room A14-204 (in-person) and online via Zoom
* Audience: Advanced undergraduates and graduate students
* Prerequisites: Basic programming (e.g., Python), introductory finance/financial economics, basic statistics and probability
* Language: Chinese

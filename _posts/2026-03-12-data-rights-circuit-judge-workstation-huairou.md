On March 12, 2026, the Beijing Internet Court's "Data Rights Circuit Judge Workstation" (数据权益巡回法官工作站) was launched in Huairou Science City to help safeguard the construction of a scientific research data zone (科研数据专区).

As part of the event, three researchers from the Beijing Institute of Mathematical Sciences and Applications (BIMSA) gave topical reports: Han Liyan (韩立岩), Researcher and Ph.D. advisor at BIMSA and Chairman of Qingyan Technology (Beijing) Co., Ltd., spoke on AI empowerment; I spoke on data governance; and postdoctoral researcher Liu Jin (刘瑾) spoke on open sharing. Together, the three talks outlined the technical logic and development blueprint for building the research data zone, while also offering forward-looking input for refining the relevant legal rules and governance framework.

On October 27, 2026, a cross-sector connection event between private equity institutions and tech-finance researchers was held at the Beijing Institute of Mathematical Sciences and Applications (BIMSA).

Three of the institute's researchers shared recent results: Feng Zijian (冯子健) presented on macroeconomic cycle network mapping; Zhang Qi (张琦) presented on stochastic volatility and primary market valuation; and I presented on quantitative investment strategies.

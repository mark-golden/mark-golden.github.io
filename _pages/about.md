I am an Assistant Professor at the Beijing Institute of Mathematical Sciences and Applications (BIMSA), affiliated with the Digital Economy Lab.

My research focuses on artificial intelligence applications in finance and economics, including fintech (digital currencies), quantitative investment, business intelligence analysis, and data asset research. I have published in journals such as *Information Sciences*, *Expert Systems with Applications*, *Knowledge-Based Systems*, *International Journal of Accounting Information Systems*, and *Technology in Society*, and I currently serve as Principal Investigator on a project funded by the China Postdoctoral Science Foundation.

Before joining BIMSA as faculty, I was a postdoctoral researcher at Tsinghua University and BIMSA (2023-2025). I received my Ph.D. in Management Science and Engineering (2017-2023) and B.S. in Information Management and Information Systems (2013-2017), both from Chongqing University, where I graduated ranked 1st out of 28 in my cohort (top 5%) and was recognized as an Outstanding Graduate.
